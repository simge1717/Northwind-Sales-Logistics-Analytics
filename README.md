# Northwind Sales and Logistics Analytics

## About the Project

In this project, I used SQL Server to analyze Northwind's sales and shipping data. I wanted to understand shipping delays, compare shipping companies, and identify areas that need improvement.

## What I Analyzed

- Late shipment rates
- Shipping company performance
- Order values and freight costs
- Customer, employee, and product performance
- Shipping time distributions and operational risks

## Key Findings

- Analyzed 830 orders, with an overall late shipment rate of 4.57%.
- Orders worth $5,000 or more had a higher delay rate.
- United Package had the highest delay rate among the three shipping companies.
- Beverages generated the highest sales among product categories.

## Tools & SQL Techniques

- MSSQL Server
- Joins, aggregations, and subqueries
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Window functions
- KPI and late shipment rate calculations
- Percentiles (P90), quartiles, median, and distribution analysis
- Shipping time and freight cost analysis
- Rule-based operational risk scoring
- Outlier and high-risk group analysis

## Recommendations

Based on my analysis, I suggested monitoring high-value orders, flagging orders that have not shipped by day 9, and reviewing shipping company performance to help reduce delays.
